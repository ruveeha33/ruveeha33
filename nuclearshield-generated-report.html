6. Detection methodology

Passive network evidence

Zeek/Suricata-style records represent mirrored metadata. The platform compares declared values with baselines without injecting traffic.

Safety integrity evidence

Logic, firmware and configuration states compare with an approved baseline represented in the evidence file. Evidence crosses outward conceptually through a data diode.

Physical-cyber and MC&A correlation

Access authorization, device identity, material accounting and radiation records are correlated by shared actor or asset. The demo does not infer real material movement.

AI-assisted anomaly analysis

A robust median and median-absolute-deviation model identifies peer-group outliers when at least five comparable numeric records exist. Results include confidence and contributors. For at least 20 comparable numeric network observations, scikit-learn Isolation Forest fits on the current upload and produces per-record decision scores and outliers; an outlier is not a confirmed attack. Neither model can take action, and human and safety authorization are required.

7. Defense measures

  1. Preserve independence: keep monitoring outside safety functions and export safety evidence in one direction.
  2. Validate before escalation: confirm evidence quality, sensor health, plant state and authorized work context.
  3. Protect provenance: retain hashes, trusted timestamps, access records and an attributable chain of custody.
  4. Authorize narrowly: require qualified cyber, operations, safety and safeguards roles according to the event.
  5. Recover predictably: use approved procedures, signed baselines, tested rollback and post-event effectiveness review.

10. Limitations and decision authority

The dataset, identifiers and values are fictional. The rules are illustrative and not validated for nuclear operations. The platform does not inspect real packets, verify a physical data diode, control safety equipment, track real nuclear material or replace qualified authorities.

Required disposition: Qualified people interpret each finding, confirm context and apply only approved safety-preserving procedures.
